About Blind Assist

Blind Assist was built from the ground up prioritizing universal accessibility standards (WCAG AAA). All interactions feature keyboard shortcuts, high-contrast visual cues, large tactile buttons, and synthesized voice feedback.

Privacy and safety are fundamental: camera feeds and geolocation data are processed on-demand with your explicit authorization.
